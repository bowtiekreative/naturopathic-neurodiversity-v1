About this directory

Finding care shouldn't mean explaining your brain from scratch every time.

We list naturopathic doctors across the US and Canada who have real training in neurodevelopmental care, and who treat neurodivergence as a difference to support, not something to fix.

What "neurodiversity-affirming" means here

Every doctor listed agrees to these in writing.

Support, not cures

Goals are about sleep, digestion, energy, anxiety and comfort. No one here promises to cure autism or ADHD.

You set the pace

Sensory needs, stimming, shutdowns and communication preferences are respected in every visit.

Part of your team

Doctors work alongside your family doctor, pediatrician, psychiatrist or therapists, and never ask you to stop prescribed care.

How we vet every listing

Each application is reviewed by a person, not an algorithm. Listings are rechecked every year.

  1. 1

    License check

    We confirm an active ND or NMD license with the regulator in each state or province where they practice.

  2. 2

    Training in neurodevelopmental care

    Doctors show continuing education in areas like ADHD, autism, PDA, sensory processing or learning differences.

  3. 3

    A written commitment

    They sign on to the three principles above. If we hear concerns from patients, we follow up and may remove a listing.

What naturopathic care can help with

Day-to-day things that make life harder: sleep, digestion and feeding, energy, anxiety, and sensory comfort. Visits are usually longer than a typical medical appointment, so there's time to talk.

What it doesn't replace

Diagnosis, prescriptions you already rely on, therapy, or emergency care. Rules on what NDs can do vary by state and province, and each profile lists where a doctor is licensed.
